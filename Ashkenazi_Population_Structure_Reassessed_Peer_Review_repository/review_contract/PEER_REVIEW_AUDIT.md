# Peer Review Audit Notes

## What is strong

- The thesis is falsifiable at the panel-design level: restore and remove central Mediterranean comparators and inspect fit/rank changes.
- Global25 and AADR are kept separate as input data streams.
- Headline claims have traceable tables and scripts.
- AADR includes primary and strict-pass sensitivity checks.
- The package explicitly avoids converting descriptive outputs into qpAdm-style source proportions.

## What reviewers may challenge

- Global25 is not a formal demographic model.
- Modern comparator labels are proxies and not literal ancient sources.
- AADR panel size for Ashkenazi is small, so genotype evidence is strongest as a sensitivity/ranking result.
- Raw genotype binaries are not stored in GitHub; rebuild instructions and manifests must carry that burden.
- Formal qpWave/qpAdm modeling remains future work.
