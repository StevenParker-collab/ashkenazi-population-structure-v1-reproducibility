# Limitations and Model Boundaries

- Global25 uses population-average coordinate data. It cannot audit upstream individual-level marker QC or LD treatment.
- Global25 coefficients are constrained coordinate-space similarity weights, not ancestry percentages.
- AADR PCA, FST, and ADMIXTURE are descriptive genotype-panel analyses.
- ADMIXTURE components are latent clusters, not literal ancient source populations.
- The AADR Ashkenazi target group has seven samples in both the primary and strict-pass panels; genotype results are reference-panel sensitivity evidence, not high-precision population-wide parameter estimates.
- No qpAdm or qpWave result is claimed in this manuscript or repository.
- Formal modeling requires separately justified candidate sources, right populations/outgroups, feasibility checks, p-values, coefficient stability, standard errors, and nested-source competition.
