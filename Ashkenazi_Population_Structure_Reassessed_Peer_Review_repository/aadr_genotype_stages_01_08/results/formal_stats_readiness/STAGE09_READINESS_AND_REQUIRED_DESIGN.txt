Stage 09 formal-statistics readiness note

The completed modern panels are sufficient for descriptive PCA, FST, and ADMIXTURE sensitivity analyses, but a defensible qpWave/qpAdm stage cannot be generated automatically from these 18 modern populations alone. Formal modeling requires a predeclared and justified set of distal right populations/outgroups, explicit source hypotheses, and appropriate ancient or deeply divergent references drawn from the parent AADR dataset.

The next formal-statistics package should therefore be constructed only after the available AADR population labels and source files are audited. It should include: (1) a reproducible extraction of target, candidate sources, and right populations; (2) SNP-overlap and missingness checks; (3) rotating and nonrotating qpAdm designs where justified; (4) qpWave distinguishability tests; (5) model competition and nested-source testing; (6) coefficient, standard-error, p-value, and feasibility filters; and (7) primary-versus-strict sensitivity comparisons.

No qpAdm result has been fabricated or inferred from ADMIXTURE components. The generated modern population inventory records the current panel and can be used when designing the formal-statistics extraction.
